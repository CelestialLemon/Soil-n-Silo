# Clearday

The design system for Soil n Silo's HUD and menus, chosen in the UI revamp (October 2026). Frosted chalk cards with
big soft corners float over the world, gauges fill as goals approach, and the screen stays nearly empty so the valley is the
thing you look at. Calm and adult rather than cheerful: muted mineral colours, light large figures, ink for the one thing to do.

**Status:** in use. Every screen of the game is built from these classes; the game's own layout on top of them is
`src/ui/game.css`.

## Files

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `tokens.json` | The source of the design tokens: colours, type, spacing, radii, shadows, timing. Edit this one. |
| `tokens.css` | Generated from `tokens.json` (`python3 tools/clearday/tokens.py src/ui/clearday/tokens.json Clearday src/ui/clearday/tokens.css`): custom properties, including the type styles as `--type-<name>` (and `-size`, `-line`, `-weight`, `-tracking`), which the `cd-` classes read. |
| `clearday.css` | The components as plain CSS classes, prefix `cd-`, built on the tokens. Hand-written. |
| `icons.svg`, `icons.css` | The illustrated icon sprite and its classes (`cd-i cd-i--<name>`, size with `--s`), generated by `python3 tools/clearday/icons.py cd src/ui/clearday "#2a3530"`. |
| `fonts/` | Manrope, bundled (Latin and Latin Extended, variable 300–800), with its licence (SIL Open Font License). |

Load order: `tokens.css`, `clearday.css`, `icons.css`. Everything sits under a `.cd-root` element. The classes need no
framework: the UI's `h()` helper applies them directly. Manrope is bundled in `fonts/` (the `@font-face` rules at the top of
`clearday.css`).

## Principles

- **Don't play the game for the player.** No plans, bills of materials, "first hour" steps, bottleneck answers or "one more X would…" suggestions before or during play. Screens state facts; the player works out what to do. Analysis belongs after the game, on the results screen.
- **Find problems in the world.** Problems show as markers over buildings. A problems strip exists only as an experimental opt-in setting.
- **Projections are optional.** "Done by" and gold/silver pace ticks appear only with the Show pace setting (off by default).
- **Keep the HUD quiet; heavy data gets its own place.** Power has its own panel. Production chains and lead times live in the guide. Building rates live in the inspector, never as labels over the map.
- **No hotkeys printed in the HUD.** Keys are listed on the pause screen and appear in tooltips.

## Content fundamentals

- Short, plain, second person, British spelling. Sentence case everywhere except the small uppercase labels.
- Numbers carry units and a direction: `+4.5 wheat / min`, `68 → 53 in 10 min`, `dry at 02:40`.
- Letters from the settlements may be warm and personal; the interface stays short.
- No emoji. Every good, building and control has a drawn icon.

## Visual foundations

**Surfaces.** `.cd-card`: chalk at 94% with a 14px blur, `r-card` corners and the `float` shadow. The one dark card on a screen (`is-ink`) holds the thing in hand (the placement forecast). Recesses are `cd-inset`. Full-screen panels are `.cd-sheet` over a blurred world.

**Colour.** `chalk` and `ink` carry the interface. Meaning colours are muted: `pine` progress and working, `ochre` now and in hand, `tide` information, water and night, `rust` danger, `plum` unreachable. Gold, silver and bronze appear only for pace and medals.

**Status.** Every status pairs a tint with a shape: circle working, half circle waiting, square blocked, triangle no power, drop dry, ring unreachable.

**Icons.** The illustrated set drawn flat with no outline, in muted colours. They sit straight on chalk; control glyphs are ink.

**Type.** Manrope only. Light 300 for large figures and names (`display`, `figure`); bold 700 for titles and values; 800 uppercase for labels.

**Shape.** Cards `r-card` 22, tiles and insets `r-tile` 16, everything else a pill or a circle. Goals are 270° gauges.

**Motion.** Cards grow out of their anchor with `spring`; gauges settle; sheets glide.

**Focus.** A 3px `ochre` outline, offset 3px.

## Layout model (in play)

- **Commission card** (top left): name, clock, goals as gauges.
- **Readouts and time** (top right): pills for credits, power and charge; the dial with the speed control and sun and wind chips; panel buttons (Ledger, Routes, Power, Guide).
- **The world**: building markers, the placement forecast on the ghost, toasts for the results of actions.
- **Dock** (bottom centre): category segments over a row of building tiles.
- **View bar** (bottom left): the overlays.
- **Side card** (right, 480px) for the inspector and routes; **sheets** for the ledger, power, guide, pause and results.

## Components

Each is a class (or a few) in `clearday.css`, used by the screens in `src/ui/`.

| Component | Classes | What it is for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Card | `cd-card`, `is-ink`, `cd-inset` | The one surface: frosted chalk floating over the world. The ink card is the thing in hand. |
| Sheet | `cd-sheet`, `cd-sheet__head` | A full-screen panel over the blurred world: ledger, power, guide, pause, results. |
| Gauge | `cd-gauge`, `__in`, `__tick`, `__foot` | A goal as a 270° arc. Pace ticks only with Show pace on. |
| Ring | `cd-ring`, `cd-ringbox`, `__in` | A share or a total as a full ring (valley health, share of time). Goals use the gauge. |
| Pill | `cd-pill`, `cd-chip` | Readouts (credits, power, charge) and small chips for goods and tags. |
| Button | `cd-btn`, `is-primary`, `is-ghost`, `is-danger`, `is-on`, `is-sm`, `is-lg`, `is-icon` | Pill buttons; ink for the one thing to do. |
| Panel button | `cd-panelbtn`, `is-on` | Opens a panel (Ledger, Routes, Power, Guide). No key badge. |
| Segmented | `cd-seg` | Speed, tabs, ranges, crop choice. |
| Tile | `cd-tile`, `is-on`, `is-poor`, `__cost` | A building in the dock. |
| Toggle | `cd-gtoggle`, `is-sm` | A good switched on or off: what a truck drops or picks up at a stop. |
| View bar | `cd-viewbar` | The map overlays as icon-and-word pills. |
| Row | `cd-row` | Label, space, value. |
| Status | `cd-status`, `is-wait`, `is-blocked`, `is-nopower`, `is-dry`, `is-unreachable` | A status as a shape, a word and a tint. |
| Problems strip | `cd-pstrip`, `__n`, `__it`, `__pop` | Experimental, opt-in: problems as shapes on the right edge. |
| Callout | `cd-callout`, `is-ink` | A label in the world on a leader line, used sparingly (route legs, stop numbers). Never building rates. |
| Toast | `cd-toast`, `is-bad` | The result of an action, gone after a few seconds. |
| Bar | `cd-bar`, `__mark` | A soft progress bar. |
| Dial | `cd-dial`, `__sky`, `__sun`, `__face` | The day as a clock face, top right. |
| Receipt | `cd-receipt`, `__row`, `__total` | What reached the depot, on the results screen. |
| Key rows | `cd-keyrows`, `cd-keyrow`, `cd-key` | The controls list on the pause screen, the one place keys are shown. |
| Medal | `cd-medal`, `is-silver`, `is-bronze`, `is-none` | Gold, silver, bronze. |
| Charts | `cd-chart` (`.axis`, `.grid`) | Text and axis colours for inline SVG charts. |
